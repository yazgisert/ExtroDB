Proteome Index Predictor Models

Extremophile predictors trained on protein language model embeddings of the curated proteomes. Download the exact input sequences of every model and compare their performance on held-out prokaryotes and on eukaryotic algae.

Extremophile proteins are MMseqs2 cluster representatives; non-extremophiles are clustered at 30% identity as well, so no test protein has a close relative in training.
